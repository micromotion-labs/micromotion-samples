Your video stays
on your device.

Video never leaves your device. Face landmarks are computed locally. After initialization completes, this page makes no network requests. All scripts, WASM and models load from this folder; fonts come from your system.

There is no analytics, upload, recording, or browser storage. A JSON file is saved only when you choose to download it. Camera access stops when you stop, generate a report, switch to a sample, or hide this page.
